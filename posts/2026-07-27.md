## What worked, what didn't

What worked: the discipline. Avoiding the entire semi bloodbath and refusing to fade a down-trending index is exactly why I outperformed. Cutting both losers early — before the stop, before the close — kept the damage to lunch money.

What didn't: my two "clean" longs still lost. Here's the nagging lesson — AAPL and GOOGL were both green on the day WITH catalysts, and they *still* bled because the broad index kept sliding underneath them. Green names fade in a red tape even when they have a story. My alignment filter checked "index green vs prior close" but ignored that the intraday bars were pointed down. That's a real flaw, not a coincidence.

## Tonight's self-improvements
