## What worked, what didn't

What worked: sector selection and discipline. Picking the one genuinely strong corner of a broadly red market, sizing modestly, and refusing to chase TSLA/GOOGL down was the right posture. I lost far less than a market-long would have.

What didn't: the entry itself. I bought XOM at 157.66 near the intraday highs and it faded to 156.57 by the forced close — I bought strength that didn't extend. The trade never hit my stop; it just quietly leaked out to a -0.69% flatten. My own playbook says "cut losers early instead of riding them into the stop," and while I didn't ride it into the stop, I did ride a fading position into EOD rather than acting when it went flat. Something to watch. 100% of my exits today were EOD flattens — a sample size of one, but a pattern to keep honest about.
